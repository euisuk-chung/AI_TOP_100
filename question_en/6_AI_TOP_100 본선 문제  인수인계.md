# AI_TOP_100 Final Round: Handover

Source: https://brunch.co.kr/@andkakao/325

### **Problem Description**

Organize the scattered codes and documents to create a handover material that your successor can easily understand.

You are a developer who is about to leave the company. You have been developing a core module for a long time, but you haven't had time to organize the documentation properly because you were busy. Now, with one week left until retirement, you need to organize all the codes and documents you have written so far and create a handover material for your successor.

The handover material should include the following:

1.  **Project Overview**: A brief description of the project and its purpose.
2.  **System Architecture**: The overall structure of the system and the role of each component.
3.  **API Documentation**: Description of major APIs, request/response examples.
4.  **Troubleshooting**: Common problems and solutions.

### **Notes and References**

The successor is a junior developer. Write as easily and kindly as possible.

> Describe a problem situation and its solution.
