# AI_TOP_100 Preliminary Round: The Secret of the Ancient Ruins

Source: https://brunch.co.kr/@andkakao/317

### **Problem Description**

Decipher the characters from the stone tablet image to obtain text, interpret its meaning, and submit it in the correct format. You are one of many adventurers who gathered after hearing rumors about the secret of the ancient ruins. At the entrance of the ruins, you found a strange stone tablet engraved with unknown characters and symbols, and next to it was a riddle: "Only when you stop will you see." According to legend, this tablet contains a spell for self-reflection, and shouting the spell reveals the tablet's secret. Find the secret hidden in the tablet and solve the mystery of the ancient ruins!
