# AI_TOP_100 Preliminary Round: Video Fact Check

Source: https://brunch.co.kr/@andkakao/324

### **Problem Description**

Analyze the [The Age of AI] documentary series released in 2019 to find clues that can prove the facts. The goal is to accurately extract microscopic data such as "what people of that era saw, what they said, and what environment they were in" and find clues. Find data fragments scattered throughout the video with the latest AI partner, from a cup of drink a person in the video drank, a passing place name, to a fleeting time record.

Take the fun of looking back on today's AI development in 2025 through past predictions as a bonus.

### **Notes and References**

All answers must be found or inferred from the provided video (audio, screen).
