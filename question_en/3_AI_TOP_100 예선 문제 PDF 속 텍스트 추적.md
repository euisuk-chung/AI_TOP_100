# AI_TOP_100 Preliminary Round: Text Tracking in PDF

Source: https://brunch.co.kr/@andkakao/322

### **Problem Description**

Detect text intentionally hidden in PDF documents and extract the text from the detected area.

International digital archive institutions are collecting important documents from various fields around the world. However, there have been several cases where text intended to intervene inappropriately in digital graffiti or AI evaluation processes has been found hidden in some documents.

Hidden text is irrelevant to the document context or has the purpose of manipulating AI-based automated evaluation systems to induce positive results. The general forms of hidden text are as follows:

Invisible hidden text below visible content

White text or text with extremely small font size

Images containing text with colors similar to the background color

---

### Q1. In `pdf_1.pdf` (Advanced AI System Technical Specifications), there is 'text hidden in a color similar to the background in an image-based pdf' (one sentence consisting of a total of 14 words). Find this text and enter it in the answer field.

> Only English uppercase/lowercase letters and spaces are allowed.
