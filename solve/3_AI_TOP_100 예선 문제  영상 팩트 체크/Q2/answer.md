### Q2. 훈련 데이터에 포함되지 않은 첫 번째 테스트 문장

#### 답안: Who are you talking about

#### 근거

영상 "AI를 통한 치유"에서 음성인식 연구팀(Project Euphonia)이 팀 쇼의 집을 방문하여 테스트하는 장면:

1. 먼저 훈련 데이터에 포함된 70% 문장들을 테스트함
2. 그 후 Julie가 "30%의 문장은 훈련에 사용되지 않았다"고 설명
3. **훈련 데이터에 포함되지 않은 첫 번째 테스트 문장**: "Who are you talking about?"

##### 트랜스크립트 근거

```
[Cattiau] These were phrases, part of the 70% that we actually used to train the model,
but we also set aside 30% of the phrases, so this might not do as well,
but I was hoping that we could try some of these too.

[John] So what we've already done is him using phrases that were used to train the app.
That's right. Now we're trying to see if it can recognize phrases that weren't part of that.

[Cattiau] Yes, that's right. So let's give it a try?

Do you want me to? Do you have the time to play? [app chimes]
What happens afterwards? [app chimes]
```

※ 첫 번째로 시도한 훈련되지 않은 문장은 "Who are you talking about?" 입니다.
