# Question
Agents give shallow replies that skip important steps.

# Answer
Chain of Thought

# Explanation
Agents may give shallow replies because they jump straight to an answer and skip the steps in between. Chain of Thought fixes this by letting the agent think out loud, breaking the problem into small, clear steps. This slows the agent down just enough to show its reasoning, catch mistakes, and produce answers that are deeper, clearer, and more accurate.
